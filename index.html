Scope and evidence

This is a broad first-pass global research map, not every work ever published. Papers and repositories were located and triaged; full texts were not all read, data were not downloaded, code was not run and accuracy was not reproduced. Approximate-title and “lead” entries need bibliographic completion before formal citation. Vendor claims, preprints and special-hardware methods are flagged.

The competition target

From ordinary RGB facial video: heart rate → heart rate + SpO₂ → a clinically benchmarked real-time system adding systolic/diastolic blood pressure. The supplied official PDF requires at least two robustness constraints at baseline-report stage.

C1 low light · C2 skin-tone diversity · C3 designated target among multiple faces · C4 low resolution · C5 low bandwidth/offline · C6 ages 60–70+ · C7 masks.

The official PDF controls requirements. Sensor ground truth or training supervision does not establish permission to use extra sensor inputs at inference.

What the research means for our team

  1. Start HR with GREEN, CHROM and POS. Establish held-out-person results before adding neural complexity.
  2. SpO₂ requires usable saturation variation. Investigate VIPL-HR, SUMS, LADH, PhysDrive and VitalVideos; isolate facial RGB input from finger/IR fusion.
  3. BP is the largest technical uncertainty. Prioritize ALIVE, PPG-to-rPPG transfer and realistic evaluation. A model outputting two numbers does not demonstrate transferable BP evidence.
  4. The integrated product idea already exists. Differentiate through measured robustness, failure detection, transparent validation and efficient RGB-only processing.
  5. Early constraint candidates: C4 and C7. Resolution/mask stress tests are approachable; test C5 separately because temporal compression can damage morphology.

Data access is a research dependency.

Several datasets require faculty-signed agreements or academic-only use. The supplied competition PDF also says submitted work may be made available to MathWorks for commercial use. Clarify permission boundaries before submitting restricted raw data or derivatives. VitalVideos counts differ by paper/site release; verify the actual accessible subset.

Evaluation checklist, search limitations and next step

Keep all clips and augmentations from one person in one partition. Verify reference timestamps, waveform versus spot-reading labels, personal calibration and inference inputs. Compare BP/SpO₂ to training-mean and demographic-only baselines. Report bias, MAE/RMSE, agreement, subgroup errors, output coverage and end-to-end latency.

Sources searched include CVF, arXiv, PubMed/PMC, publishers, institutions, original GitHub projects, dataset providers, vendors and RePSS proceedings. This was a scoping search, not a systematic review; exact rejected-hit counts were not retained. Gaps include non-English work, inaccessible papers, private systems, complete patent families, organizer-paper full texts and directly verified vitiligo-specific validation.

Next step: build the data/label eligibility inventory and reproduce three classical HR methods under clean, resolution and mask conditions. Broad skill areas: DSP, vision, physiology, ML, statistics, synchronization, efficient coding and reproducibility.
