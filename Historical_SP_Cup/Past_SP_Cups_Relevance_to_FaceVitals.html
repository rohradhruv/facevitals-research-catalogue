Closest match: 2015 heart-rate tracking

Wrist-PPG under strong motion interference offers useful denoising, candidate selection and temporal tracking ideas. Its contact/accelerometer inputs differ from facial RGB, and it does not solve SpO₂ or blood pressure.

2017 adds real-time periodicity tracking; 2016 adds weak-signal/illumination context; 2018 suggests device/domain tests; 2026 offers system-integration lessons. No earlier task reviewed here combined all three FaceVitals outputs from RGB facial video.

Evidence limits: official year descriptions were reviewed; not every legacy technical PDF or private solution was recoverable. Located code was not executed. A later dataset paper is not automatically a winning submission.

First useful experiment

With the same facial RGB extraction and evaluation split, compare independent dominant-peak HR estimates against confidence-aware temporal candidate tracking. Test motion harmonics, output coverage and lag during changing HR. Video-derived motion references are a hypothesis to test, not a replacement proven equivalent to wrist acceleration.
